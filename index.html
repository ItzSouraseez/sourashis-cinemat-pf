How a film gets made

Brief, frame, final cut.

  1. Act I — Direct

    The brief becomes a shot list

    A finance channel needs a topic to feel urgent; a school needs its week to feel big. I write the angle, the script beats and the boards before a camera is unpacked.

  2. Act II — Shoot

    Light, lens and the take

    On set I light and operate. Talking heads that hold attention for ten minutes, and event days shot run-and-gun with 300 builders in the room.

  3. Act III — Deliver

    Shot for the edit, out on time

    Coverage planned around the cut, so the editor has every shot the story needs and a weekly upload date holds.
